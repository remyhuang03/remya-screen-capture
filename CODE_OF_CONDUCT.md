# Code of Conduct

Everyone is welcome to participate in Remy'a Screen Capture. Treat contributors
and users with respect. Give feedback about the work, be patient with different
levels of experience, and respect people's privacy and boundaries.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention,
and sharing someone's private information without consent are unacceptable.
These rules apply to project issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces
where people participate in or officially represent the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer at
[me@remya.top](mailto:me@remya.top). Include links or dates if available and tell
us if there is a safe way to contact you. Please avoid posting reports in public
issues. If the concern involves the maintainer or you cannot safely contact them,
you can [report the content or account to GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

The maintainer will review reports, share information only as needed to respond,
and protect the reporter's identity as far as practical. Responses may include
removing content, issuing a warning, or restricting participation, depending on
the severity and whether the conduct has happened before. Retaliation against
people who report concerns or help review them is unacceptable.
